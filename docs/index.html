EPL Match Predictor

Win, draw and loss probabilities for every Premier League match, from a Poisson goals model written from scratch. Each forecast is locked before kickoff, and after the final whistle it is scored against the result and the bookmakers.

Models fitted on Premier League results and shots on target since 2015/16. Match data and odds from football-data.co.uk. A study project by Dhruv Upadhyaya, MIT Manipal. Probabilities are estimates, not certainties, and this is not betting advice.
